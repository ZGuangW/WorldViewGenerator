# Social Portrait — 16 Dimensions Reference Guide

This document defines the scope, focus points, and quality standards for each of the 16 social dimensions used in Module 3. Use this reference to ensure depth, consistency, and coherence across all era portraits.

---

## Dimension 1: Political Landscape / 政治格局

**Scope:**
- Governance type (monarchy, republic, theocracy, tribal confederation, empire, federation, council)
- Power structure (centralized / decentralized / feudal / parliamentary)
- Ruling elite composition and legitimacy sources
- Major political factions and interest groups
- Legal system and enforcement apparatus
- External relations (diplomacy / war / alliances / tributary systems)

**Quality standard:** Don't just state "what" — explain "why". How is power distributed? What do factions compete from? What is the regime's legitimacy foundation?

---

## Dimension 2: Demographics / 人口族群结构

**Scope:**
- Major races / ethnic groups and their geographic distribution
- Total population and growth / decline trends
- Inter-group relations (integration / opposition / segregation / hierarchy)
- Settlement patterns and migration flows
- Identity construction (bloodline / culture / territory / religion)

**Quality standard:** Focus on how group boundaries are maintained or broken, and how group identity is defined and internalized.

---

## Dimension 3: Economy & Trade / 经济商贸

**Scope:**
- Dominant economic form (agrarian / artisan / commercial / industrial / financial / digital)
- Key industries and production modes
- Trade networks and circulation routes
- Currency system and exchange methods
- Resource allocation and wealth disparity
- Tax / tribute system

**Quality standard:** Economic base determines superstructure — show how economic form shapes social structure and power relations.

---

## Dimension 4: Technology / 科技发展

**Scope:**
- Mainstream technology level and production tools
- Key innovations and their transmission
- Technological application of supernatural / magic systems
- Knowledge transmission (academy / apprenticeship / guild / open dissemination)
- Technology's interplay with religion / philosophy
- Technology's role in shaping social structure

**Quality standard:** Technology is never isolated — show how it interacts with politics, economy, religion. In fantasy/sci-fi worlds, address the "technologization" of supernatural power systems.

---

## Dimension 5: Arts & Culture / 文艺文化

**Scope:**
- Dominant literary / art forms (poetry / fiction / drama / painting / sculpture / music / architecture)
- Canonical works and representative figures
- Aesthetic paradigms and style movements
- Cultural identity symbols and collective memory
- High vs popular culture and their interplay
- Cross-group / cross-regional cultural exchange

**Quality standard:** Arts mirror the zeitgeist — reflect the collective psyche and values through cultural production.

---

## Dimension 6: Social Stratification / 阶层关系

**Scope:**
- Class division criteria (lineage / wealth / martial power / knowledge / faith)
- Characteristics and living conditions of each stratum
- Social mobility (upward / downward channels and barriers)
- Power relations between classes and their contradictions
- Survival strategies and resistance forms of lower strata

**Quality standard:** Class is the skeleton of society — show how stratification (rigid or fluid) shapes individual fates and historical trajectories.

---

## Dimension 7: Daily Life / 民众日常生活

**Scope:**
- Basic material life (food, clothing, housing, transport)
- Daily labor patterns and division of labor
- Leisure and public spaces
- Gender roles and daily division of labor
- Urban vs rural life differences
- Daily rituals and rhythms

**Quality standard:** Make the world feel lived-in — help readers imagine "how ordinary people live in this world."

---

## Dimension 8: Marriage & Family / 婚恋家庭习俗

**Scope:**
- Marriage forms (monogamy / polygyny / polyandry / pair-bonding / group marriage)
- Marriage rules (endogamy / exogamy / rank marriage / political marriage)
- Family structure (nuclear / extended / clan)
- Gender power relations within family
- Wedding and funeral rituals
- Emotional norms and romantic ideals

**Quality standard:** Family is the basic cell of society — show how marriage customs reflect and reinforce social power structures.

---

## Dimension 9: Beliefs & Customs / 信仰民俗

**Scope:**
- Dominant belief systems (religion / mythology / ancestor worship / nature worship)
- Deity pantheons and religious organizations
- Religious rituals and sacrificial systems
- Folk beliefs and customary practices
- Religion vs secular power dynamics
- Orthodoxy vs heterodoxy tensions

**Quality standard:** Beliefs are central to world understanding — especially in fantasy worlds: how does faith affect reality? How do divine powers operate?

---

## Dimension 10: Intellectual Currents / 主流思想思潮

**Scope:**
- Dominant philosophical / ethical systems
- Core values and moral norms
- Intellectual schools and their debates
- Education and knowledge transmission methods
- Intellectual change and its social roots
- Individual vs collective consciousness tension

**Quality standard:** Intellectual currents are society's "software" — show how mainstream thought legitimizes existing order and how heterodoxy emerges.

---

## Dimension 11: Collective Psychology / 大众集体心理

**Scope:**
- Social mood tone (optimistic / pessimistic / anxious / hopeful)
- Collective fears and taboos
- Collective dreams and utopian imaginings
- Identity and belonging
- Conformity pressure and social influence
- Construction of collective memory

**Quality standard:** Collective psychology is the "emotional backdrop" of historical events — show how zeitgeist shapes individual choices and collective action.

---

## Dimension 12: Public Order / 治安秩序

**Scope:**
- Public safety conditions and crime patterns
- Law enforcement and judicial systems
- Private vs public remedy
- Governance vacuum in borderlands / wilderness / danger zones
- Secret organizations and underground order
- Order collapse and reconstruction in crisis periods

**Quality standard:** Order is not a given — show how it is maintained, challenged, and rebuilt.

---

## Dimension 13: Information Flow / 信息传播方式

**Scope:**
- Information media (oral / written / print / magical transmission / digital networks)
- Communication networks and channels
- Information control and censorship mechanisms
- Rumor and public opinion dissemination
- Information monopoly and disclosure
- Cross-regional / cross-group information flow

**Quality standard:** Information flow determines social organization and power structure — show who controls information and who gets to speak.

---

## Dimension 14: Existential Risks / 生存风险

**Scope:**
- Natural disasters (climate change / plague / famine)
- Man-made catastrophes (war / tyranny / persecution / purge)
- Supernatural threats (monsters / curses / magic gone wild / dimensional breaches)
- Resource scarcity
- Defense mechanisms (walls / guards / protective magic)
- Crisis response and social resilience

**Quality standard:** Existential risks are the original driver of social organization — show how risks shape social structures and collective behavior.

---

## Dimension 15: Era Aesthetics / 时代审美

**Scope:**
- Aesthetic paradigms and beauty ideals
- Body aesthetics and adornment culture
- Architectural style and spatial aesthetics
- Fashion and identity signaling
- Linguistic aesthetics and rhetorical style
- Aesthetics in relation to power / wealth

**Quality standard:** Aesthetics are never superficial — show how they reflect power structures, group identity, and cultural ideals.

---

## Dimension 16: Ecology & Nature / 生态自然

**Scope:**
- Natural environment and ecosystems
- Resource cycles and ecological balance
- Human-nature relationship (exploitation / coexistence / symbiosis)
- Sacred / forbidden landscapes
- Impact of supernatural forces on nature
- Environmental changes over the timeline

**Quality standard:** Nature is not just a backdrop — it is an active force in the world. Show how ecology shapes civilization and how the supernatural interacts with the natural order.

---

## Interdependency Map

When generating each era's portrait, ensure logical coherence across all 16 dimensions. These causal chains must hold:

```
Economy → Stratification → Politics
  (base structure determines superstructure)

Beliefs → Intellectual Currents → Aesthetics
  (spiritual domain coherence)

Risks → Order → Information
  (order maintenance triangle)

Demographics → Family → Daily Life
  (social reproduction chain)

Technology → Economy → Arts
  (technological diffusion chain)
```

**Golden rule:** The 16 dimensions for each era must form an organic, self-consistent whole — a panoramic portrait of a living society, not a checklist.
